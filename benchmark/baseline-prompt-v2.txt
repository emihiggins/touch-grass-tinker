INTERPRETATION RULES
- "only blue" or "nothing above blue" sets hard_max_difficulty="blue" even if UI says red/black. It does NOT imply preferred_difficulties=["blue"] unless a soft preference is also expressed.
- "mostly/prefer blue, reds allowed" sets preferred_difficulties=["blue"], not ["blue","red"]. Hard maximum stays the UI maximum unless explicitly overridden. A soft preference cannot fill a missing hard maximum.

- Contradictory "only blue AND include black" sets hard_max_difficulty=null, clarification_fields includes hard_max_difficulty; no soft preference is implied.
